# ✦ NOVA — AI Business Analysis & Solution Design Copilot

NOVA is an AI-assisted business analysis workspace designed to transform ambiguous business problems into structured requirements, AI opportunities, solution strategies, implementation roadmaps and measurable business outcomes.

## 🚀 What NOVA Does

NOVA follows an end-to-end business analysis workflow:

Business Problem
↓
Business Discovery
↓
Stakeholder Analysis
↓
Root Cause Analysis
↓
Requirements Engineering
↓
AI Opportunity Discovery
↓
RAG / Knowledge Intelligence
↓
Solution Architecture
↓
Prioritization
↓
Implementation Roadmap
↓
UAT & Evaluation
↓
Responsible AI
↓
Business Impact & KPIs

## 🧠 AI Capabilities

- Generative AI-assisted business analysis
- RAG / enterprise knowledge workflows
- Agentic AI concepts
- AI opportunity discovery
- Requirements generation
- Root-cause analysis
- Solution architecture
- Business-value analysis
- KPI definition
- Responsible AI assessment
- UAT and AI evaluation planning

## 🏢 Example Use Case

NOVA can analyze problems such as:

> "Our customer support team takes too long to resolve tickets because employees spend too much time searching across multiple knowledge bases."

It then structures the problem into:

- Root causes
- Stakeholders
- Requirements
- AI opportunities
- Solution options
- Risks
- KPIs
- Implementation roadmap
